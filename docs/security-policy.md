Email Security Policy
1. Purpose
This policy defines security requirements for organizational email systems.
2. Scope
The policy applies to:
- Employees
- Contractors
- Email administrators
- Organizational email accounts
- Corporate email systems
3. Account Security
Users must:
- Use strong unique passwords.
- Enable MFA where available.
- Never share passwords.
- Report suspected account compromise.
- Lock or sign out of devices when required.
4. Phishing Protection
Users should verify unexpected:
- Links
- Attachments
- Login requests
- Payment requests
- Password reset messages
Suspicious messages should be reported through the organization's approved reporting process.
5. Sensitive Information
Confidential information must only be transmitted through approved methods.
Users must not send sensitive organizational information to unauthorized recipients.
6. Encryption
Encryption should be used when required by information classification or organizational policy.
Approved encryption technologies should be maintained and reviewed by security administrators.
7. Email Authentication
The organization should implement:
- SPF
- DKIM
- DMARC
- MFA
8. Administrative Access
Administrative privileges should follow least-privilege principles.
Administrative actions should be logged and reviewed.
9. Incident Reporting
Users must promptly report:
- Phishing
- Malware
- Account compromise
- Accidental data disclosure
- Suspicious email activity
10. Training
Employees should receive periodic security awareness training.
11. Policy Review
This policy should be reviewed periodically and updated when technology, threats, or regulatory requirements change.
12. Conclusion
A clear email security policy establishes consistent security behavior and supports technical controls.
