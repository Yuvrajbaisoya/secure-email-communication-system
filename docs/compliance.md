Compliance Considerations
1. Introduction
Email security controls may support an organization's legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
The exact requirements depend on the organization's location, industry, data types, and applicable regulations.
2. GDPR
Organizations handling personal data of individuals covered by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) should consider:
- Data confidentiality
- Data integrity
- Access control
- Data minimization
- Security of processing
- Incident response
- Appropriate technical and organizational measures
3. HIPAA
Organizations subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) may need controls for protecting electronic protected health information.
Email systems handling such information should be assessed against applicable organizational and regulatory requirements.
4. Logging and Monitoring
Security logs should be protected and retained according to applicable policy and legal requirements.
5. Data Protection
Organizations should identify:
- What data is sensitive
- Where data is stored
- Who can access it
- How it is transmitted
- How long it is retained
6. Access Control
Least privilege and MFA should be used to reduce unauthorized access.
7. Incident Response
An organization should maintain procedures for:
1. Detection
2. Investigation
3. Containment
4. Recovery
5. Notification where legally required
6. Lessons learned
8. Important Note
This document provides security planning considerations and is not legal advice. A qualified legal/compliance professional should determine the exact regulatory obligations.
9. Conclusion
Compliance should be considered during system design rather than treated as an afterthought.
