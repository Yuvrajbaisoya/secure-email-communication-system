Employee Email Security Training
1. Purpose
Employees are an important part of email security. This training provides basic guidance for identifying and responding to common email threats.
2. Recognizing Phishing
Be cautious of messages containing:
- Unexpected links
- Unexpected attachments
- Urgent requests
- Unusual payment requests
- Requests for passwords or MFA codes
- Suspicious sender addresses
3. Links
Before opening a link, verify that the destination is legitimate and expected.
Do not enter credentials into suspicious login pages.
4. Attachments
Do not open unexpected attachments simply because they appear to come from a familiar organization.
If uncertain, verify through an approved communication channel.
5. Passwords and MFA
Never share:
- Passwords
- MFA codes
- Recovery codes
Use MFA whenever provided by the organization.
6. Sensitive Information
Do not send confidential information to unauthorized recipients.
Use approved encryption and secure communication methods when required.
7. Reporting
Report suspicious messages to the organization's designated security team.
Early reporting can reduce the impact of phishing and malware incidents.
8. Quick Checklist
Before trusting an email:
- Check the sender.
- Check the context.
- Check links carefully.
- Treat unexpected attachments cautiously.
- Never share authentication codes.
- Report suspicious messages.
9. Conclusion
Security awareness combined with technical controls provides stronger protection against email-based threats.
